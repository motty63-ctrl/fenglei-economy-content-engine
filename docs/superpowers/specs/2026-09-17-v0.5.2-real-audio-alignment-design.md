# V0.5.2 Real Audio Alignment Specification

Status: design for human review; no implementation or model execution authorized by this document.

## Scope and invariant inputs

Align the already approved 14-sentence GDP narration to its existing real WAV. The audio SHA-256 is `3bf65eec73b721f79de32980cbc9333752c137b1b1cb14559916a0a790af7330`; the recorded audio duration is 60,611 ms. Re-check the bytes at execution time; these values are not a substitute for validation. Do not regenerate narration, script, storyboard, or audio. Do not run timeline, renderer, or MP4 stages.

`NarrationProvider` and `AlignmentProvider` remain independent. Production alignment never uses deterministic duration, character-count allocation, fake timestamps, or a fake fallback. No diarization and no ASR-generated rewrite of approved text.

## Provider boundary

`AlignmentRequest` contains `audio_path`, expected `audio_sha256`, actual WAV `duration_ms`, the approved `NarrationDocument` (ordered `sentence_id`, `original_text`, `narration_text`), and the matching voice review. The orchestrator validates WAV/metadata/quality/review hashes before passing the request. The provider reads audio but does not mutate it, the narration, or any upstream artifact.

`AlignmentProvider.align(request) -> AlignmentResult` returns one candidate per sentence with measured `start_ms`, `end_ms`, `text` equal to the input `narration_text`, raw acoustic score or an explicit missing-score error, `score_source`, `method`, `provider`, and model ID/revision. It also returns independently recognized ASR text and word/character mapping evidence for consistency checks. Provider-specific output stays inside the adapter. The provider cannot register a valid artifact or set the production threshold.

## Local provider selection

First opt-in provider: local WhisperX on CPU or an explicitly selected compatible device, with a pinned Chinese CTC alignment model and a separately pinned local ASR model. Use the approved `narration_text` as the forced-alignment reference. ASR is only an independent audio-to-reference consistency check and source of coarse speech regions; it does not replace, correct, or write back the script. No diarization. Dependency/model installation and downloads are isolated in an optional environment; default offline tests use recorded-shaped fixtures and require neither models nor network.

The adapter must reject missing or interpolated sentence boundaries, unknown model revisions, unalignable text, missing acoustic scores, and contradictory ASR/reference matches. Raw model score is recorded as `confidence` with `confidence_source`; it is **not** described as a calibrated probability. WhisperX's default Chinese model is a starting point, not an acceptance guarantee for GDP/BEA/API and numeric speech. A failed local canary may lead to a separately reviewed provider change, not a fake fallback.

## Matching-only deterministic text normalization

Keep `original_text` and `narration_text` byte-for-byte unchanged. Derive `normalized_ref` and `normalized_asr` only for monotonic matching and text-consistency scoring; persist their values and normalization rule version in candidate diagnostics, never in approved script artifacts.

Rules are ordered and versioned: Unicode normalization; remove only matching-irrelevant Chinese/ASCII punctuation and whitespace; case-fold English; map explicitly enumerated equivalent spoken/written forms for GDP, BEA, World Bank, years, numbers, percentages, and units. Equivalence must preserve numeric value, year, unit, institution, and order. Unknown acronym or numeric variants are mismatches, not guessed equivalents. Tests include `2.8%` versus `百分之二点八`, `2024年` versus `二〇二四年`, and negative cases such as `2.8%` versus `2.9%` or a different year. Normalization never produces timing; model-observed acoustic alignment does.

## Artifact and review lifecycle

Keep `alignment.json` owned by `audio_alignment`. Add `alignment_candidate.json` owned by `audio_alignment` for model output and diagnostics, and `alignment_review.json` owned by `alignment_review` for the 14 manually labeled sentence boundaries and calibration decision. Candidate depends on approved audio, quality, review, and narration; review depends on candidate and current audio SHA; final alignment depends on candidate and review. The registry tracks hashes and propagates stale downstream. Before review, `alignment.json` remains stale and cannot feed timeline.

`alignment.json` schema version `5.2` retains the current top-level timing fields and adds `audio_sha256`, `audio_duration_ms`, `provider`, `method`, `model_id`, `model_revision`, `confidence_source`, `confidence_threshold_id`, `coverage_ratio`, text-match summary, and `review_hash`. Each sentence requires `sentence_id`, unchanged `text` (= `narration_text`), integer `start_ms`/`end_ms`, measured `confidence`, `confidence_source`, `provider`, `method`, and current `audio_sha256`. Historical 5.0 fake artifacts remain readable for audit but are never production-eligible. Do not write a partially valid final artifact after any failure.

## Quality gate and calibration

Structural production gate is always fail-closed: exact 100% ordered sentence coverage with no duplicates; `0 <= start_ms < end_ms <= actual WAV duration`; monotonic starts and ends; no overlap; unchanged narration text; all SHA links and voice approval match; production audio quality valid; no fake method/fallback; no missing or interpolated measurements. Real trailing silence is allowed: do not stretch the last sentence to equal audio duration. Unexplained large gaps, inconsistent ASR/reference content, or missing acoustic evidence fail rather than being filled using duration proportions.

Do **not** hard-code the old `0.85` as a permanent threshold for uncalibrated CTC scores. The first GDP run is a calibrated canary: a human marks the start/end of all 14 sentences, reviews audible text and pauses, and marks each model boundary acceptable or unacceptable under a documented boundary-error tolerance. Record model scores beside human boundary errors; derive a *candidate* threshold and record its model revision, voice, normalizer version, sample size, and observed false-accept/false-reject counts. Human approval of a measured threshold is required before promotion, and every sentence must meet that threshold. Fourteen sentences cannot establish a general production threshold; any approved threshold is scoped to this canary/model/voice. If the sample cannot support a defensible threshold, mark calibration `insufficient` and do not promote `alignment.json`, even with human approval of the audio; gather additional labeled alignment examples or change the aligner under a new review. Future automatic runs remain fail-closed until separately validated calibration exists. Never lower structural gates to pass the canary.

## Minimal pipeline change and acceptance

Add a standalone `audio_alignment` command/path that only reads approved narration/audio artifacts and writes candidate; a separate review/promotion action may write `alignment_review.json` then final `alignment.json`. Neither path invokes upstream generation or timeline. Existing timeline consumer receives the same ordered integer-millisecond sentence spans only after final artifact is valid. Acceptance requires real local model output, 14 human-reviewed boundaries, SHA-bound review, all structural gates, documented threshold scope, and default offline suite unchanged.
