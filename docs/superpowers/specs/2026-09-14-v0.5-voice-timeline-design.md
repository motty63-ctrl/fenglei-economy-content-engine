# V0.5 Voice, Timeline, and Nikola Adaptation Specification

## Scope

V0.5 converts already-valid `script.json` and `storyboard.json` into normalized narration, canonical audio, sentence alignment, an audio-authoritative timeline, and a deterministic Nikola renderer project. It does not create a final MP4 or alter content, facts, angles, beats, or storyboard semantics.

## Stages and ownership

1. `narration_generation` owns `narration.json` and `narration.txt`.
2. `audio_generation` owns `audio/narration.wav` and `audio/metadata.json`.
3. `audio_alignment` owns `alignment.json`.
4. `timeline_compilation` owns `timeline.json`.
5. `nikola_adaptation` owns `renderer_project/` and `render_manifest.json`.
6. `render_preflight` owns `preflight_report.json` and `render_qa.json`.

Every artifact retains owner, hash, dependency hashes, timestamps, and status in `run.json`. Changed input hashes make descendants stale; stale inputs cannot be silently consumed.

## Contracts

The canonical audio format is mono PCM signed 16-bit WAV at 24 kHz. `audio/metadata.json` records format, codec, sample rate, channels, duration in integer milliseconds, SHA-256, provider, voice ID, and optional native timestamps.

Narration normalization may change pronunciation only. Each sentence records `sentence_id`, `original_text`, `narration_text`, reasons, and reversible operations. A deterministic semantic gate ensures numbers, percentages, years, and units retain their values.

`NarrationProvider` accepts normalized sentences plus a voice ID and returns canonical audio bytes and optional native timestamps. `AlignmentProvider` independently accepts narration plus audio metadata and returns sentence timestamps and confidence. The alignment gate requires exact 100% sentence coverage, monotonic order, no overlap, no bounds violations, exact audio-end agreement, and confidence at or above the configured threshold; a separate fallback provider may be used.

`timeline.json` uses integer milliseconds. Sentence times come only from real audio alignment; beat and scene bounds are derived from their mapped sentence IDs. V0.4 duration estimates remain references and are never used to stretch or allocate audio.

Nikola adaptation is a deterministic translation of valid storyboard and timeline data. It preserves scene/object IDs, provenance, lifecycle, layout, transitions, and directives; it cannot add facts, characters, metaphors, or change values. Unsupported directives fail explicitly.

Renderer preflight validates schema, Node, HyperFrames, browser, FFmpeg, FFprobe, fonts, SVG/assets, timeline, and directives. Dry-run frames and temporary videos live only in a temporary directory and are deleted; only capability results are written to QA artifacts. A full render is forbidden in V0.5.

## Recovery

Stages are independently restartable. Valid outputs are reused by default; `--force-stage` rebuilds one owner stage. Provider/voice changes explicitly invalidate the affected output chain. A failed alignment preserves valid audio, and a failed preflight preserves the renderer project for diagnosis and retry.
