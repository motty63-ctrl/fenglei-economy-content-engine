# V0.3 Angle Scoring and Eligibility Policy

Each score dimension is an integer from 0 to 5. `evidence_strength` and `controversy_risk` are computed locally. The deterministic offline planner supplies the other five planning scores using the rubric below. These scores describe planning quality; they are not evidence and do not verify claims.

| Dimension | Weight | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `evidence_strength` | 25 | Eligible supported claims, verification basis, and distinct institutional independence keys where the basis is independent corroboration |
| `audience_relevance` | 20 | How directly the framing follows an explicit Research focus or legacy research question |
| `novelty` | 15 | Structural distinction from sibling proposals in the same planning batch |
| `hook_strength` | 15 | Whether the hook is a concise question that identifies the framing's subject |
| `visual_potential` | 5 | Whether the proposal has multiple supported claims or a multi-document claim suitable for a later comparison visual |
| `explainability` | 20 | Whether the number of supported claims fits a short, scoped explanation |
| `controversy_risk` | penalty | Locally detected conflict framing; 0 means no conflict signal and 4 means a prohibited fabricated conflict |

```text
base_score = evidence_strength*5 + audience_relevance*4 + novelty*3
           + hook_strength*3 + visual_potential*1 + explainability*4
total_score = max(0, base_score - controversy_risk*6)
```

The base score is 0–100. `total_score` ranks candidates for display and may provide a system recommendation. It does not select an angle or create `angle.md`; human selection remains a separate owner stage.

## Deterministic offline planning score rubric

For proposals generated by `offline-generic-angles-v1`:

- `audience_relevance` is 4 when the request has a validated `research_focus`, otherwise 3 for the legacy question path.
- `novelty` is 3 for each distinct structural strategy generated in a batch. Duplicate questions, mechanisms, takeaways, or framings fail the separate diversity gate.
- `hook_strength` is 3 when the hook is phrased as a question, otherwise 2.
- `visual_potential` is 3 when the proposal supports multiple claims or one claim spanning multiple source documents, otherwise 2.
- `explainability` is 4 for one to three supported claims and 3 for four or five.

These values are deterministic planning heuristics, not subjective facts. Other providers may propose scores within the model's 0–5 bounds, but provider scores cannot waive claim, attribution, scope, evidence, originality, conflict, or diversity checks. Eligibility is assigned locally.

## Evidence strength

Evidence scoring uses distinct supported claim IDs and `independence_key` values from source rows marked `counts_as_independent=true`. Raw source IDs are never treated as institutions.

- 0: no usable claim with an accepted basis.
- 2: at least one usable claim with `independent_corroboration` or `authoritative_primary_attestation`.
- 3: at least two usable claims, or one/more independent-corroboration claims supported by at least two distinct independence keys.
- 4: at least three usable claims and at least two distinct independence keys among independent-corroboration evidence.
- 5: at least three usable claims and at least three distinct independence keys among independent-corroboration evidence.

An authoritative primary attestation is a legitimate evidence basis for the exact attributed document statement or deterministic document comparison. Multiple documents with the same independence key remain one independent institutional source; authority attestation does not increase the run's `independent_source_count`.

## Local eligibility and diversity gates

An eligible candidate requires `evidence_strength >= 2`, `explainability >= 3`, and no hard-rejection reason. The current runtime does not impose separate eligibility thresholds on `audience_relevance`, `hook_strength`, or `controversy_risk`; these dimensions contribute to the display score. Fabricated conflict is independently hard-rejected.

Hard rejection applies to unknown or unsupported claims, a missing verification basis, repeated claim references, fabricated conflict, source reuse, duplicate angles, authority attribution/scope expansion, insufficient evidence, or `explainability < 3`. Authority-backed text must preserve the attested attribution and scope; deterministic document comparisons must preserve the attested measures, period, statistic, unit, certainty, and values. Unsupported causality, motive, market impact, or projection-to-commitment expansion remains ineligible regardless of score.

The proposal batch must contain 3–5 candidates. At least three eligible candidates must have distinct core questions, hooks, hook mechanisms, audience takeaways, and narrative framings. Narrative framing labels are open values; no fixed GDP-oriented set is mandatory. These checks establish structural distinction rather than requiring named story types.

After eligibility, the system may rank candidates deterministically by `total_score`, evidence strength, audience relevance, explainability, novelty, and `angle_id`. Ranking is only a recommendation. The `angle_selection` owner remains separate.
